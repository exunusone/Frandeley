# Frandeley Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

An editorial music film for independent artists: deep studio blacks, crisp white typography, honest creative moments, and cobalt blue used as a precise signal. The work should feel human, focused, and quietly ambitious. Preserve the current Frandeley dark studio direction and make the pre-launch status explicit. Photography may feel cinematic, while layouts stay clean and readable.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#070B10`
- Raised surface: `#0D141C`
- Primary text: `#F8F9FB`
- Secondary text: `#B2BFCB`
- Signal blue: `#0962FF`

## Typography

- `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` for display and body copy, matching the site.
- Heavy white headlines; restrained uppercase labels with generous tracking.

## Motion

- Editorial entrances with short, confident movement and varied easing.
- Use a drawn cobalt rule or waveform as the primary motif.
- Scene changes use a restrained push or dissolve; motion stays legible and purposeful.
- Keep the video silent, easy to pause, and short enough to load on mobile.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not present Frandeley as a launched or usable product.
- Do not claim distribution, AI, or specific product functionality is available.
- Do not show invented dashboards or illegible fake UI as a real screen.
- Avoid neon overload, generic gradients, visualizer clichés, and stock-photo posing.
- Do not include real unreleased music or imply the site accepts audio uploads.
